# Raining Hell — working notes

## After every game change
Rebuild and repackage so the playable app and DMG in `dist/` always match the source:

```sh
./scripts/build.sh     # updates dist/Raining Hell.app and runs all tests
./scripts/package.sh   # rebuilds dist/Raining Hell-macOS.dmg and the portable ZIP
```

Don't run `scripts/release.sh` unless asked; it overwrites the versioned candidate in `dist/releases/`.

## Feature notes (keep in mind when touching related code)

- **RPG gunner is manually aimed (implemented).** `TroopKind.rpg` has `manualAim`: it never takes `Soldier.target`. `World.attack` routes to `World.fireRockets` when `rocketsSelected` (every selected soldier is an RPG); in mixed selections RPGs are skipped and hold fire. The aim line is `World.rocketTrajectory(of:at:)` toward the pointer, drawn in `GameView.drawTrajectories` only while `aimingRockets`. Rockets are `Shot`s with `rocket: true` and their own weaker `gravity`. Reload is deliberately not shown on the map (only the menu text and an RPG RELOADING status line).
- **Fire simulation (`Sources/Fire.swift`, drawing in `Sources/FireView.swift`).** Firebomb runs start cell-by-cell fires; see the `FireField` doc comment. Weather affecting fire is planned and handed off in `HANDOFF-fire-weather.md`.
- **Water simulation (`Sources/Water.swift`, drawing in `Sources/WaterView.swift`).** Levels' `pools` are only seeds: `World.seedWater` flood-fills them into a live `WaterField` (mass per cell). See the `WaterField` doc comment: sideways flow is driven by each column's head (`surfaceLevel`) with momentum (`flux`), and what crosses between full columns is taken off and `deposit`ed onto the column tops, so water stays incompressible; the vertical rule never packs excess downward. Only active cells step; settled water is woken through its `rim` check, so code that removes voxels needs no water hook, but code that *places* a voxel in water must call `displaceWater`. Rubble (10) is porous (`rubblePorosity`); soil, rubble, sandbags and crates soak water (`absorbency`, darkening via `wet` in the terrain tile cache). Blasts call `splashWater`, chunks `chunkInWater`; splashes use the water's own RNG so `World.random` sequences (and the tests) are unchanged. `--water-preview dam|splash|surface|drone|bridge [s]` renders scenes with `--screenshot`. Every level's water must lie still at rest (physics test).
- **Support aircraft fly in from the nearer map edge, or the side the player picks.** Supply planes, gun-run jets and firebombers carry a `heading` (+1 east, -1 west) from `World.approach(toward:)`: `World.supportSide` if set, else `World.heading(toward:)` (nearer edge). While support is being placed, Q forces west and E east (pressing the active side again returns to auto); `GameView.placing`'s `didSet` clears the choice when support placement ends. Ghosts and `strafeFloor` use the same accessor. Flight height follows the skyline: `World.skyline` (top of the tallest standing block, re-measured every 0.5 s and at each call-in) minus `flightClearance` gives `transportAltitude`, capped at `flightCeiling` (60 px below the top of `World.skyHeight`, the open sky the camera can rise into) so the camera can still reach the planes. Transports ease toward it in flight (`towardCruise`), so they descend when a tall structure falls. The jet takes `jetCruise` (90 px higher) at call-in, and `strafeFloor` raises its dive until no part of the pass clips a building. They spawn beyond that edge via `World.entry` and are removed once they've flown off the far side (`World.departed`). Anything they release (crate drift, canister velocity, gun muzzle, burst walk direction) and all their drawing, minimap arrows and call-in ghosts follow `heading`. Jet rounds fired from off-map start where their flight path crosses the map edge.
- **Building collapse and rebar (`Sources/Structure.swift`).** `layRebar` casts bars (`World.rebar`, materials `barH` 13 / `barV` 14) through all map concrete (not bridge or brick); blasts and fire bare them and they keep bridging holes but buckle under load. `World.reinforcement` is the per-level toughness knob (1 = first level; later levels raise it): it scales rebar concrete's blast resistance, capacity, span and tension. Each solve first runs `toppleStructure`: storey cuts weigh the weight above against the tension bonded cells can hold, and an overbalanced section becomes a `rooted` hinged chunk that creaks over, crushes wreckage under it, then `snapped` breaks it at its weakest storey. Big level sections dropping onto lower floors pancake through them (`pancakes`), and torn building sections only hang from joints above their middle, so failed supports drop a building straight down rather than tipping it. Keep the intact map at zero collapses (first physics test).
- **Rebindable keys (`Sources/Keybinds.swift`).** In-game actions are `KeyAction`s matched by physical key code through `Keybinds.shared` (saved under `keybinds` only when `persistent`). They are rebound on the settings CONTROLS tab, in both the lobby and the pause menu. `GameView.perform(_:)` runs them. Menu hotkeys, Esc and 1-9 stay fixed. Any on-screen hint that names a game key should use `k(.action)` (or `Keybinds.shared.label` in `World`) and not a literal letter.
- **Operations (`Sources/Levels.swift`, see `LEVEL-CONSTRUCTION.md`).** Six levels in `LevelSpec.all`, each wider, taller (`lift`), better reinforced and with a higher requirement and its own grant. Map size is per level: `World.select(i)` resizes `World.columns`/`rows`, and every World must be `reset` afterwards. Kessel Crossing uses the original sector builders in `Levels.swift`; each later front is its own country with unique landmarks in `Fronts.swift`, built from the pieces in `Landmarks.swift`. Never reuse one front's landmarks on another, and read the solver and troop rules in `LEVEL-CONSTRUCTION.md` before adding structures. Level 1 (Kessel Crossing) must stay voxel-identical to the original. Levels unlock in order via `World.cleared`. Keep the `-wmo` flag in `build.sh`.
- **Kamikaze drone (`Sources/Drone.swift`, decision in `docs/decisions/drone-implemation.md`).** Support row 4. It flies in from the run-in edge to the placed point, then follows the pointer with quadcopter physics (tilt-limited thrust, gravity, drag; constants on `World`). `GameView.steeringDrone` feeds `World.steerDrone`, and `GameView.followDrone` keeps it in frame with a dead zone. It detonates on contact, on a field click while piloted, on `KeyAction.detonate`, or falls once its battery dies. Water never sets it off: touching water (`droneInWater`) shorts it out (`shortOutDrone`), freeing the drone slot, and the dead airframe sinks and rests in `World.wrecks`. Only one flies at a time.
- **Upgrade shop (planned).** A lobby shop that spends `World.bank` on permanent upgrades (shorter support cooldowns, more troop damage, bigger magazines) is handed off in `HANDOFF-shop.md`. Route upgraded stats through one `World` accessor so drawing and firing agree.
